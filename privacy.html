Privacy Policy

Farm Village · Last updated 10 November 2026

Farm Village is a farming game that runs entirely on your device. This page explains what the game does and does not do with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Farm Village has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send your farm, your choices or any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Your saved game

Your farm is saved on your own device, in the app's local storage. It stays there until you start a new farm, clear the app's data, or uninstall the game. The optional save code in Settings is a text copy of your farm that you can copy yourself to move your game to another device. It is never uploaded.

Neighbors

The neighbors, visitors and orders in the game are made-up characters. They are not other players, and the game has no chat or contact with other people.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it runs. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address and browser details to deliver the font files. See Google's privacy policy. After that the fonts are kept on your device so the game also works offline.

Permissions

The game asks for no special permissions. It may use vibration for tap feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Farm Village is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game's GitHub page: github.com/caspxau/farm-village/issues.
